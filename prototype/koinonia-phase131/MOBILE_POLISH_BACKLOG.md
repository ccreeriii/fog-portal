# KOINONIA — Mobile Polish & Ergonomics Backlog
*Approved Future Enhancements Deferred to Consolidated Mobile Polish Pass*

The Product Owner and team have identified and approved the following UX, visual, and control refinements. These items are intentionally deferred from Phase 0.13 to prevent cosmetic distraction from core gameplay loop and navigation engine development.

---

## Backlog Items

1. **Joystick Position Tuning:**
   - Move the virtual analog joystick slightly farther right (e.g. `left: 28px` or `32px`) to provide safer left-thumb travel and prevent unintentional edge-swiping on bezel-less phones.

2. **Consolidated Settings Modal:**
   - Move Sound toggle (Mute/Unmute) and Reset Prototype state inside a unified Settings gear icon/modal to declutter primary gameplay headers.

3. **Header Spacing & Logo Breathing Room:**
   - Clean up header padding and element distribution so the KOINONIA lockup and sub-brand ("Fire of God Ministries Virtual Community") have permanent uncompressed visual prominence across compact screens.

4. **Life Points Icon Update:**
   - Replace the current heart/numeric icon with a distinctive green sprout / life seedling symbol aligned with the spiritual growth metaphor (*"A virtual world that grows when you grow in real life"*).

5. **Official Final Koinonia Logo Asset:**
   - Integrate the exact vector/SVG final lockup asset once finalized by creative brand leadership.

6. **Consolidated Typography & Component Tuning:**
   - Uniform audit of card paddings, modal drag handles, pill typography, and button tap heights for tactile consistency across iOS and Android.

---
*Status: Approved for subsequent dedicated Mobile Polish Phase.*
