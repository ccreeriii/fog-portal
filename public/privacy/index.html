1. Who We Are

Fire Of God Ministries operates the Fire Of God Ministries Community Portal at fogmin.site. The Portal supports ministry participation, member connection, events, attendance, faith formation, and community communication.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the Portal, we may handle account details, name and contact information, age or birthday, profile information, ministry and event participation, attendance, registrations, messages you choose to send, notification subscriptions, challenge or game activity, and technical information needed to keep the service secure and working.

3. Sensitive Personal Information

Some ministry records may reveal or strongly indicate religious affiliation or participation in religious activity. Age and minor-related information may also require heightened protection. We limit access to this information according to ministry responsibilities and use it only for appropriate Portal and ministry purposes. We do not sell sensitive personal information.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, maintain member records, operate events and attendance, support ministry participation, provide communications and notifications, respond to support and privacy requests, protect the Portal from misuse, and meet lawful ministry and administrative responsibilities.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the activity and applicable Philippine law, processing may be based on your request or agreement, legitimate and proportionate ministry or organizational purposes, compliance with legal obligations, protection of vital interests, or consent where consent is the appropriate basis. Acknowledging this Privacy Policy does not replace consent where separate consent is legally required.

6. Google Sign-In and Google User Data

Google Sign-In uses normal authentication information from the OpenID, email, and profile scopes. The Portal does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Contacts, or other Google services. Google user data is not used for advertising, and we do not sell Google user data.

7. QR Codes, Member Identification and Attendance

A member QR code or identifier may be used to find a member record and record attendance. It is an identification aid, not an account password. Do not share a private password or misuse another person’s identifier.

8. Events, Ministries, Challenges and Community Activity

The Portal may record event registration and attendance, ministry applications and roles, faith-formation progress, challenge participation, game scores, badges, prayer or community activity, and related history. Visibility varies by the nature of the feature and the permissions of the person viewing it.

9. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared with authorized ministry workers who need it for their role, with service providers that help operate the Portal, when required by law, or when necessary to protect people, the ministry, or the service. We seek to disclose only what is reasonably needed.

10. Security

We use administrative and technical safeguards such as access controls, protected credentials, encrypted security-email queues, and operational backups. No online service can provide an absolute security guarantee, so please use a strong private password and report suspected misuse promptly.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate ministry records, security, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Retention depends on the kind of record and its continuing purpose; this policy does not promise one numeric period for every record.

12. Children and Young People

The ministry community includes children and young people. We aim to use age-appropriate practices, limit access, and involve a parent, guardian, or authorized ministry leader where appropriate. A parent or guardian may contact us about a young person’s information.

13. Notifications and Communications

If you enable notifications or request account email, the Portal uses the information needed to deliver it. You can disable browser notifications through your device or browser. Essential account-security messages may still be sent when appropriate.

14. Cookies, Browser Storage and Similar Technologies

The Portal uses a secure session cookie for sign-in and may use browser storage and service-worker caching for expected features such as preferences and offline read-only access. Sensitive authentication routes are not intended for offline replay. Clearing site data may sign you out or remove locally cached information.

15. International Service Providers

Some technology providers, including hosting, email, authentication, notification, or security services, may process limited information outside the Philippines. We use providers for defined operational purposes and consider appropriate safeguards and provider terms.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, object to or restrict certain processing, request deletion where appropriate, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a privacy concern. Some rights have lawful exceptions.

17. Account Correction and Deletion Requests

For V1, send correction or deletion requests to support@fogmin.site. The Portal does not promise a self-service deletion feature. Deleting a login account does not automatically require deletion of every lawful ministry record, such as an attendance or safeguarding record that must appropriately be retained.

18. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

We investigate suspected incidents and take reasonable containment and recovery steps. Where applicable law requires notification to affected people or authorities, we will provide it through appropriate channels.

19. Third-Party Websites and Content

The Portal may link to third-party sites or services. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies, and Fire Of God Ministries is not responsible for content or practices outside the Portal.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the Portal, ministry practices, or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Future re-acceptance for material changes may be introduced separately.
