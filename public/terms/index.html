1. Purpose of the Community Portal

The Fire Of God Ministries Community Portal supports a safe, respectful Christian community through member connection, events, attendance, ministries, formation, communications, and faith-centered activities.

A Portal login may connect to an existing ministry member record through an authorized Account Claim invitation. Do not use another person’s invitation or attempt to redirect a member record. Ministry records and login accounts serve related but distinct purposes.
