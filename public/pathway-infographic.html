🌱 7-Stage Growth Pathway

The Organic Discipleship Blueprint (Click any phase to explore)

1

Encounter "Come & See"

▼
The Goal

To invite individuals to explore faith at their own pace without feeling overwhelmed or institutionalized.

  • Targeted Seeker Funnels (URL Query Routing)

    Specialized entry points (?play=, ?read=, ?event=) mapping to the Wanderer, Spiritual Seeker, and Community Seeker.

    Why it belongs: Provides frictionless, customized entry based on where a person is spiritually. Avoids heavy 10-page forms.

  • Rich Guest Dashboard (Daily Manna SPA)

    A standalone guest application featuring the Liturgical Daily Mass card, Reflection hook, and Prayer Request form.

    Why it belongs: Delivers immediate spiritual value to build trust. Gentle conversion hooks prompt account creation.

  • Faith Quest Challenge

    Christian arcade mini-games (e.g., Emoji Sermon Translator) available in Guest Mode.

    Why it belongs: Uses gamification to draw in the casual Wanderer. Earning XP prompts 1-tap Google Auth saving.

  • Event Pre-Registration System

    A portal for RSVPing to low-barrier events like the Alpha Youth Series.

    Why it belongs: Handles the logistical phase seamlessly, capturing email data before they even walk through physical doors.

2

Connect & Belong "Find your circle"

▼
The Goal

Anchoring members into Cell Prayer Groups for shared life, proving they are not walking alone.

  • The Campfire (Groups Hub)

    Why it belongs: True fellowship happens between Sundays. Provides a 24/7 digital living room for the cell group to bond.

  • Memories Photo Feed & Optimistic UI Reactions

    Why it belongs: Captures modern social media engagement but redirects it toward holy fellowship. Accelerates vulnerability.

  • Secret Prayer Pal CRON Engine

    Why it belongs: Forces organic, one-on-one spiritual intimacy. Ensures no one feels invisible.

  • Prayer Wall & Intercession Counter

    Why it belongs: Tangibly demonstrates community support by showing how many are actively praying for specific burdens.

3

Step In Commitment / Pledge

▼
The Goal

Moving members from passive attendees to committed members of the spiritual family.

  • The 7-Stage "Growth Pathway" UI

    Why it belongs: Eliminates spiritual ambiguity. Actively calculates exact milestones and displays personalized pastoral quotes.

  • Dynamic "My Journey" Dashboard Module

    Why it belongs: Digitizes the commitment pipeline, explicitly commanding the immediate next physical action.

  • Phygital Commitment Rites (Framework)

    Why it belongs: Bridges physical altar calls with the digital CRM, instantly updating database stages upon a digital pledge.

4

Discover Your Gifts Discernment

▼
The Goal

Helping members unpack their God-given talents and formally express their intent to serve.

  • "Intent to Serve" Ministry Application

    Why it belongs: Formalizes the calling pathway. Paper forms get lost; digital intents route directly to the leadership dashboard.

  • Full Community Directory & Ministry Hub

    Why it belongs: Allows users to explore church needs, discover where gifts fit best, and designate a Priority Ministry.

  • Audition & Consultation Pipeline

    Why it belongs: Specialized ministries require evaluation. The Google Meet Video Vault integration locks auditions to calendars.

5

Equip & Form Formation

▼
The Goal

Deepening roots through structured servant formation, character building, and strict accountability.

  • QR Scanner Check-in & Attendance Health

    Why it belongs: Formation requires discipline. Autonomously monitors the 80% standard, flagging "Ready for Acceptance" when met.

  • Private Spiritual Journals & Daily Examen

    Why it belongs: True formation happens daily. Templates provide the architectural scaffolding for an inner spiritual life.

  • Deep Dives & Video Vault

    Why it belongs: Provides the digital classroom infrastructure for asynchronous theological training and sermon reflections.

6

Serve with Joy Participation

▼
The Goal

Actively deploying members into the harvest to build the Kingdom through practical ministry.

  • Moderation Dashboard & Granular RBAC

    Why it belongs: Serving requires logistical authority. Ensures a newly appointed servant has exact digital tools to execute roles.

  • Event Logistics & Leader's Notes

    Why it belongs: Transforms the app into an operational Command Center, allowing the active workforce to coordinate seamlessly.

  • Web Push Broadcasts

    Why it belongs: Guarantees instant, high-visibility communication for serving teams, bypassing noisy group chats.

7

Commissioned "Be Sent"

▼
The Goal

Graduating mature disciples who are now equipped to shepherd others and lead the community.

  • Silas AI Assistant

    Why it belongs: Acts as a digital staff member, querying SQLite directly to identify slipping sheep so leaders can reach out.

  • Live Analytics Dashboard

    Why it belongs: Leaders must know the health of their flock. Provides instant macroscopic visibility into engagement levels.

  • Global Leaderboards (Gamification)

    Why it belongs: Celebrates a lifetime of faithful participation, establishing a culture of joyful endurance in the faith.
