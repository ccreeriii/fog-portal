Fire Of God Ministries Community Portal

Help & FAQ

A simple guide for our community family. Find quick answers and learn how each part of the Community Portal works. This staging copy is prepared for leadership review.

Leadership preview: use “Preview as” to review what different people may see. In the final Community Portal, help topics will be shown automatically according to each person’s access.

👋

Getting Started

Everyone

The Community Portal is our shared online space for the Fire Of God Ministries family. It brings together events, member information, ministries, prayer, growth activities, games, reminders, and other helpful community features in one place.

Some parts can be viewed without signing in. An account is needed for things that belong to you personally, such as your profile, growth journey, attendance, schedule, scores, messages, and other saved activities.

Yes. You can add the Community Portal to your phone’s Home Screen so it is easier to open and feels more like an app.

👤

Account & Sign In

Everyone

Open the Sign In page and choose the option that matches your account. You may use your Community Portal account or Google Sign-In when it is available for you.

Tap “Forgot password?” on the Sign In page. Enter your email and follow the instructions that are sent to you if your account can use password recovery.

This helps protect everyone’s privacy. The Portal gives the same message whether or not an email is connected to an account.

Yes, when it is available for your account. Google Sign-In lets you use your verified Google account to enter the Community Portal.

🏠

Home Dashboard

Signed-in Members

The Home Dashboard is your starting place after you sign in. It brings together the things that may matter to you today, such as your growth journey, upcoming events, schedule, prayer, community activities, and helpful shortcuts.

Some cards and choices appear only when they are useful for your account, ministry, role, or permissions. This helps keep the Home page simple and relevant.

🌱

Growth & Planner

Signed-in Members

Growth & Planner helps you see your personal next steps and keep track of the activities that support your journey in faith, community, formation, and service.

The Planner helps you remember what is coming up and what you may want to do next. It can bring together personal activities, responsibilities, and growth-related reminders.

No. Personal information is shown only where it is meant to be shared. Some parts are for you, while other parts may be visible only to the right leaders when needed.

🛤️

My Journey & Seven Milestones

Signed-in Members

My Journey helps you see where you are in your life with the community and what your next step may be. It is meant to encourage growth, belonging, service, and mission.

The seven milestones are: Encounter (Come & See), Connect & Belong, Step In (Pledge), Discover Your Gifts, Equip & Form, Serve with Joy, and Commissioned (Be Sent).

No. Milestones are simply encouragement and guidance. They do not measure your holiness, your value, or God’s favor.

📖

Daily Gospel & Daily Manna

Signed-in Members

These areas help you return to Scripture and reflection during ordinary days, not only during gatherings. They are there to support a regular habit of listening to God’s Word.

Open the daily reading or reflection, read at your own pace, and return whenever you want to spend time with Scripture.

🙏

Prayer Center, Prayer Covenant & Prayer Pal

Signed-in Members

The Prayer Center brings together prayer-related activities available to you in the Community Portal.

The Prayer Covenant is a simple way to take part in the community’s shared rhythm of prayer and remember the people or intentions you have committed to pray for.

Prayer Pal helps members remember and pray for another person in the community when that activity is available.

Prayer information should only be shared with the people it is meant for. Avoid putting very sensitive information in a place meant for wider sharing.

✍️

Spiritual Journal

Signed-in Members

The Spiritual Journal is a personal place for gratitude, reflections, prayer notes, and thoughts about your journey with God.

No. It is meant to be a personal space. You should still avoid sharing anything there that you would not want stored in your account.

✨

Life Points & Growth XP

Signed-in Members

They are encouragement tools that can help make participation and growth activities more engaging.

No. Points, ranks, milestones, and participation records are never a measure of holiness, personal worth, or God’s favor.

When available, you can see them in the parts of the Portal connected to your growth, activities, or rankings.

💬

Silas AI Assistant

Signed-in Members

Silas is the Community Portal’s AI helper. Silas is designed to make it easier to find help, understand Portal features, and support ministry-related questions inside the app.

No. Silas is a helpful tool, but it does not replace prayer, pastoral care, spiritual guidance, community relationships, or conversations with the right people.

Use good judgment with personal information. For sensitive pastoral, family, health, or private matters, it is better to speak directly with the appropriate person.

📅

Events & Registration

Everyone

Open Events to see what is coming up, including the date, time, place, poster, and other event details.

Open the event and tap the registration button when it is available. Fill in the requested details and submit the form.

Yes. When an event has a Share Invite button, you can send it to family, friends, or other community members.

The Portal prepares an event preview so the person receiving your link can quickly see what the event is about.

QR Check-In & My Schedule

Signed-in Members

Your member QR code helps make attendance and check-in easier during supported community events and activities.

No. Your QR code helps identify your member record during check-in. Your account password should still be kept private.

My Schedule shows event responsibilities or activities assigned to you when that information is available for your account.

Where this feature is available, you can view your recorded attendance in your member area.

Campfires & Small Groups

Inbox, Announcements & Notifications

Signed-in Members

The Inbox is where you may receive messages or updates meant for you inside the Community Portal.

Announcements are community updates shared with the people who need to receive them.

Notifications can help you remember events, receive important updates, and stay connected with community activities.

Check that notifications are allowed for the Community Portal on your phone or browser. If you changed devices or settings, you may need to allow them again.

Worship Hub

Signed-in Members

The Worship Hub brings together worship resources that are available to your account, such as songs, setlists, keys, tempo information, media, and chord resources.

Some worship resources may be shown only to members who need them for their ministry or role.

✝

Faith Quest Challenge

Signed-in Members

Faith Quest Challenge is our faith-learning game area. It is for Bible, Scripture, Catechism, theology, and other Christian learning activities.

You may find quizzes, memory challenges, Bible questions, Catechism topics, and other fun ways to learn more about the Christian faith.

Yes. Some challenges may give scores, points, or rankings so members can enjoy learning together in a fun and friendly way.

🏃

Sports & Fitness

Signed-in Members

Sports & Fitness is for movement, exercise, wellness, and sports-related activities.

Yes, when a particular activity includes scoring, progress, or a friendly ranking.

🎮

FOG Arcade

Signed-in Members

FOG Arcade is the Community Portal’s digital game area for fun games and eSports-style activities.

Yes, when replay is available. After a game, you may see options to play again, return to the Arcade, save a score, or share your result.

No. Only games that are connected to scoring or rankings will appear on a leaderboard.

🪪

Profile & Member Directory

Signed-in Members

Your Profile is your personal member area. It may contain your basic information, photo, member details, and settings that are available for you to manage.

The Member Directory helps people in the community find member information that has been approved for sharing.

No. The Portal should only show information that is appropriate for the person viewing it.

↗

Sharing

Everyone

Tap the Share button when you see it. Your phone may open its normal sharing choices, or the Portal may let you copy a link.

No. A normal shared link should not include your password or private sign-in information.

📱

Install on Phone & Offline Use

Everyone

Open the Portal in your phone’s browser and use the browser’s option to add it to your Home Screen.

Some parts may still be available when your connection drops, but anything that needs fresh information or saves a new change may need internet access.

Open the Portal again and allow it to reconnect normally. If something still looks outdated, refresh the page.
